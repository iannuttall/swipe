Swipe is a short weekly email with practical lessons from using AI every day, plus prompts, tools, and links worth a click. No news, no fluff, under five minutes.

The readers are founders, marketers, operators, product people, and developers who use AI as part of how they work. Some are technical, some are not. If your product helps them build, rank, or ship faster, this is a good place to be seen.

## What you can book

**Main sponsor.** One per issue. Your own section in the body of the email, written in the same plain style as the rest of the issue, with a few sentences about your product and a link.

**Classified.** A one-line listing with a link in the classifieds section at the end of the issue. Cheap, and easy to book for multiple weeks.

## Good to know

- Only ads that are genuinely relevant to readers are accepted.
- Sponsor copy stays in the email's plain voice. No all-caps, no hype, and help writing it is included.
- Every issue is also published on the [public issue archive](/issues), so placements stay live after send day.

## Book a slot

Email [sponsor@swipe.md](mailto:sponsor@swipe.md?subject=Sponsoring%20Swipe) with what you're promoting and you'll get a reply with availability and current pricing.
