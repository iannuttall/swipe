Swipe is a weekly publication for founders, marketers, operators, product people, and developers who want useful AI ideas they can apply.

It looks for practical prompts, skills, workflows, tools, experiments, and business patterns that are good while they are still early or under-shared.

## What makes the cut

- It solves a real problem.
- There is something concrete you can reuse.
- It has a useful test, example, result, or receipt.
- It is not another generic agent harness everyone has already seen.

<Callout title="The issue test">
Every featured idea should explain what was found, why it matters now, what you can swipe, what happened when it was tested, and where it falls short.
</Callout>

Ian Nuttall writes and tests Swipe. His posts and tools may appear in the newsletter, but Swipe has its own editorial identity and archive.
