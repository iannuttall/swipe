## PilotCut

- **Type:** tool
- **Verdict:** selected
- **Discovery source:** Keep
- **Sources checked:** [landing page](https://www.pilotcut.com/), [agent editing guide](https://www.pilotcut.com/blog/edit-videos-with-ai-agents), [changelog](https://www.pilotcut.com/changelog), and [privacy policy](https://www.pilotcut.com/privacy), all primary and checked 2026-08-04

## Homebench

- **Type:** tool
- **Verdict:** selected
- **Discovery source:** recent Hacker News stream
